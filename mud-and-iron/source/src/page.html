    How the war works

    Supply comes from your HQ and every sector flag you hold. Infantry capture a flag by standing next to it with no enemies nearby. Supply Depots raise your population cap and let squads reinforce nearby.

    Engineers dig trenches (Z, then drag), string barbed wire (X, then drag), build structures (C), cut wire and repair vehicles and buildings (right-click). Buildings must go near your HQ or a sector you hold.

    Cover matters. Infantry in a trench take a fraction of the losses they would in the open. Shell holes, ruins and woods help a little. Barbed wire slows infantry to a crawl and stops armored cars, but tanks crush it.

    Suppression from machine guns and shells pins infantry: they go prone and fire poorly. Push them past their limit and they break and run for the HQ. Units earn up to three veterancy stars from kills.

    Tanks cross trenches and shrug off rifle fire, but they are slow and can break down. Field guns, grenade bundles and other tanks kill them. Armored cars are fast but cannot cross trenches or wire.

    Command powers (top right) unlock with an Airfield (recon flights, strafing runs) and an Artillery Park (gas, creeping barrage). Research gas masks at your HQ before the enemy does it to you. Rain turns the ground to mud and slows everything, tanks most of all.
